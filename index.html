Our Story

The path that brought us here

Our story began with a piano and a chance knock at the door. Michèle heard Brennan playing a Mozart Sonata and recognized it from her childhood recitals. Having not had neighbors previously, she knocked on the door to introduce herself.

Months and many countries traveled later, Michèle finally accepted an invitation to hang out on the rooftop after Brennan ran into her on the elevator following a pilates class. With no travels planned for the evening, Michèle accepted. It was there the idea was formed for a Bamba Tuesday night at Rock Steady. The night didn't end at dancing, it wasn't until after chicken wings at 4:30 am, in the most Atlanta of places, that the night concluded. Work was difficult, but one thing was for certain, everyday after that, was one we wanted to spend together!

More About Us

Meaningful moments along the way

01

Brought together by chance

Neither of us thought that we would ever end up in Atlanta. Having just finished college, Michèle was ready to travel the world. Brennan was in the grind of a master's degree. Michèle thought Brennan was just a musician. Brennan thought Michèle was dodging him, what do you mean you're going to Puerto Rico, you said you going to Costa Rica last week.

02

Tuesday Tradition

Following the infamous Bamba Tuesday, date night dinner became a Tuesday tradition. Our first date was a Tuesday evening at the great Poor Calvins. It started pouring rain when the check came. With no umbrella in hand, Brennan ran (the fastest he ever has) back to the apartment to grab one. There was no way he was going to let the rain ruin her hair. As an Italian the heart lies in the stomach. Michèle's incredible cooking made Brennan greedily proclaim, no one is ever allowed to have this peanut stew. It is the greatest meal Brennan had ever had, and will forever be the nail in the coffin.

03

The Proposal

In the beautiful country of Curaçao, the weather never drops below 80 degrees. Why then, did Brennan dress up in a full suit, and continue to take 30 minute trips- to apparently get water. The ask for a beach stroll sounded delightful, the recommendation of a fancy dress seemed peculiar. Then, after brief commentary on the large chess board, "Who's your Guy" began to play. We turned the corner, and there lay a flower paved walkway and arch, with the sign reading, "Will You Marry Me" Brennan dropped to a knee and asked Michèle if she would make him the luckiest man in the world, and join on the adventure of the rest of their lives as husband and wife. Michèle said yes, a million times yes!
